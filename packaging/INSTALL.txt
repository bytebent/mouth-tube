mouth-tube for macOS (AU + VST3 audio effect, Apple Silicon and Intel)

Easiest way
  1. Right-click "Install.command" and choose Open (a plain double-click may be
     blocked the first time, because the script isn't from an identified developer).
  2. Click Open in the warning, and wait for "Installed" in the Terminal window.

Or by hand, in Terminal, from inside this folder:
  cp -R "mouth-tube.component" ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/
  cp -R "mouth-tube.vst3" ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/
  xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/Components/"mouth-tube.component" ~/Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3/"mouth-tube.vst3"

Then in Ableton Live 11: Preferences > Plug-Ins > turn on "Use Audio Units"
and/or "Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders" > Rescan. mouth-tube appears under
Plug-Ins > Bytebent Audio. Drop it on any audio track (or after an instrument).
Use either the AU or the VST3, not both.

Presets you save are kept in ~/Music/Bytebent/mouth-tube/Presets.

"mouth-tube.app" is a standalone version for quick tests outside Ableton.

The xattr step is needed because the plugin isn't signed with a paid Apple
developer certificate; it removes the "downloaded from the internet" flag so
macOS lets Ableton load it.
